Manga Overlay Translator

A browser extension that translates raw manga as you scroll any manga site. Each speech bubble gets covered by a natural translation in your language that keeps the meaning.

The unique kernel: meaning, not word-for-word

Word-for-word (what you get today)
“How did you get the other way?”
“I live at Lagos.”
Meaning-first (what this does)
“How did you get into my house?”
“I live in Lagos.”

Translated one bubble at a time, so each line reads the way a fluent speaker of your language would say it. It doesn't need to remember the whole story.

Target language (phase one: English)

In the demo, you scroll a raw chapter on one or two real manga sites. A page that was unreadable a second ago now reads like a cleanly translated page, and switching the language changes every bubble.

Why it matters to you

“As I'm just scrolling through, you're seeing it in English, overlaid and it's making sense. That would be epic for me.”

Fan translations are often word-for-word and copied across sites (“20+ translations that are the exact same junk”), and you don't want to be “conformed to just one website.”

The boundary

Now: the proof of concept6
  • Browser extension that isn't built for one specific site, tested and demoed on one or two real raw sites
  • Finds the speech bubbles, reads the source text, and translates by meaning, bubble by bubble
  • Draws each translation over its bubble: fully covering it, sized and placed to fit, 0.95 opacity by default
  • Translates automatically as pages scroll into view
  • Simple settings: adjustable overlay opacity and a target-language picker
  • Japanese source. Phase one is English only, then Spanish and French for the demo
Later7
  • Proven reliability across many sites and layouts
  • Polished typesetting: manga fonts, curved text, matching each bubble's style
  • Sound effects and text outside bubbles
  • Caching so re-reading is instant and free
  • Korean webtoons, Chinese manhua, and other source languages
  • Fully polished dark, Crunchyroll-inspired settings UI
  • More target languages beyond English, Spanish, and French
Explicitly cut3
  • Whole-story context across chapters: the goal is that each bubble makes sense on its own, not story memory
  • Sheet-music idea: you chose manga and want more music knowledge before shaping that one
  • Standalone reader site: the point is reading on the sites people already use
